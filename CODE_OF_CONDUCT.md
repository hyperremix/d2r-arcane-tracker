# Code of Conduct

D2R Arcane Tracker is built by and for the Diablo II: Resurrected community. Everyone is welcome to take part, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation. Nobody should be harassed here.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and constructive. People have different levels of experience.
- Welcome newcomers and help them get started.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats or stalking.
- Discriminatory jokes, slurs or personal attacks.
- Trolling or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without consent.
- Spam, or passing off others' work as your own.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and docs. It also applies anywhere you represent the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainer, [@hyperremix](https://github.com/hyperremix). Reports are handled confidentially. You'll hear back within 48 hours, and a decision follows within 7 days.

Depending on severity, the maintainer may issue a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban. The maintainer may also remove or edit contributions that violate this code.
